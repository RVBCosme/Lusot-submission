**Goal:** Finish Lusot's backend for MS5's screens. Each report is stored and published by its mode, pins show their work, demo pins and samples are seeded by script, and the failure paths are tested before the pipeline freezes.

**Architecture:**
- **The API Lambda** (Node.js 22, TypeScript, Vitest) gains the Metro Manila test, the upload checks, device limits, nearby pins, the map, the feed, the pin detail with its links, flags and samples.
- **The analysis Lambda** (Python 3.13 container, pytest) gains storage by mode, publishing at write time, `keep_photo` for sample seeds, and the overlay, its layers and `readings` from MS3b's evidence record.
- **One SAM template** gains MS4's routes and the read-only `FeedApi`, the `limits` table, the `feed-index`, the literal `INCHES_SHOWN: "false"` (C515), CORS, throttles, the `demo/` backstop, C525's retentions, the alarms and the role grants.
- **Scripts** in `backend/scripts/` seed and remove demo pins and samples, hide pins, redrive the dead-letter queue and run the cloud recovery checks.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript (esbuild, Vitest, `ngeohash`, `@aws-sdk/*` 3.1157.0 plus `@aws-sdk/s3-request-presigner`), Python 3.13 (OpenCV, NumPy, boto3, pytest, moto, uv; PyYAML in the template tests), AWS SAM, DynamoDB, S3, SQS, SSM, CloudWatch.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-ms4-backend-design.md`, draft 4 (`e34a580` on `ms4-backend-specs`, which holds `9efbdcd` and `1867afe`), with Window 4's two edits since: §4.7's blur-geometry conditions and the `rim_outline` field (§4.2, C524), and the pre-flight's edits to §4.5, §4.7, C512, C524 and MS4-Q10. ## Global Constraints

- **CLAUDE.md holds throughout:**
  - **Infrastructure:** one SAM template in `ap-southeast-1` and no console change; no reserved concurrency, and `MaximumConcurrency` stays 5; no VPC; deploy as `rene-dev` with `--profile lusot` only.
  - **Privacy:** never log photos, exact locations, IP addresses or device ids; never write a key, the account ID or a console link into a file; faces are blurred before Meta, and faces and plates again before anything is stored.
  - **Copy:** no "safe" in any form, no crossing advice, and only "at this spot"; the 9 levels and MMDA's 3 classes only; the screen line word for word wherever the API serves copy.
  - **Data:** never read, list or search a folder named `sealed`, and every script that reads `lusot-data` refuses one (C66); no Set A or A-neg photo before the split; Set A photos never go into a repo or the demo.
- **Exact values (the spec):**
  - **Links and checks:** upload links and photo links last 300 s. A pin is visible 3 h and kept 7 days. GPS accuracy is 50 m or better, the nudge at most 100 m (optional, default 0), and the capture window 15 minutes.
  - **Limits (C508, C522, MS4-Q13):** per device per UTC clock hour, 10 uploads and 5 sample runs; in all per hour, 300 uploads and 30 sample runs. Refusals are `429 {"error": "device_limit"}` and `429 {"error": "global_cap"}`, each with `Retry-After` set to the seconds left in the UTC hour. A missing or malformed id counts in the shared `none` bucket. Only SHA-256 hex hashes of device ids are stored, and no report stores one (C520).
  - **Check order on `POST /uploads`:** the uploads switch (503 `uploads_paused`); the body (400 `bad_request`; 422 `capture_too_old`, `capture_in_future`, `gps_too_coarse`, `nudge_too_far`); the device limit; the global cap, whose refusal gives the device's count back. `POST /samples/{sampleId}`: the switch, the sample id (404), then the same two limits.
  - **Nearby:** 9 geohash cells of 6 characters with no sort-key range (C509); `radius` 300 m by default and at most 300; at most 200 pins, newest first, as `{"pins": [...]}`; public coordinates rounded to 4 places (C514).
  - **The map and the feed:** `GET /map/pins` gives visible published `live` and `demo` pins as `{"pins": [...]}` (C519). The feed gives `live` pins only, from `FeedApi` (any origin, GET only, 5 a second with a burst of 10), with `Cache-Control: public, max-age=60`, memoised 60 s, and the screen line as its `notice`.
  - **`GET /reports/{id}`:** a route throttle of 30 a second with a burst of 60 (C508); 404 past `expiresAt` (C526).
  - **The publish switch** acts at write time and at read time (C516). Demo pins stay. A change shows within about 90 s at the API: the 30 s switch cache plus the feed's 60 s memo, plus up to 60 s more in a client's cache that honours `max-age=60` (the map within about 60 s: the switch cache plus its 30 s memo; nearby within 30 s).
  - **Demo pins:** `visibleUntil` is the end of the `--until` day in Manila (23:59:59 +08:00), `expiresAt` 7 days later, `--until` at most 53 days ahead, and the `demo/` backstop 60 days.
  - **Queues (C525):** the dead-letter queue keeps messages 518,400 s and the upload queue 345,600 s. Visibility 1,080 s, lease 900 s, Lambda timeout 180 s, `maxReceiveCount` 5.
  - **DynamoDB:** 19 read and 14 write units in all, inside the free 25 and 25 (MS1's 15 and 10, `feed-index` 2 and 2, `limits` 2 and 2).
  - **Costs:** about $0.032 per analysed photo; MS4's cloud runs stay under $2 in all.
- **Names:**
  - **Modes:** `live`, `private`, `sample`, `eval`, `demo`.
  - **New item fields:** `published`, `feedPart`, `readings`, `overlayKey`, `layersKey`, `consentVersion`, `credit`, `place`, `event`, `seed` (an `eval` sample seed), `sampleId` and `demoSource`. MS1 already stores `gpsAccuracyM`, `nudgeM` and `flags`; MS3b adds `levelFrom`, `depthInMin` and `depthInMax`.
  - **Prefixes:** `raw/`, `blurred/`, `overlay/`, `demo/blurred/`, `demo/overlay/`, `samples/` (and `samples/manifest.json`).
  - **The API's environment:** MS1's `TABLE_NAME`, `INDEX_NAME`, `BUCKET_NAME`, `STOP_PARAM_PREFIX` and `NODE_OPTIONS`, plus `LIMITS_TABLE_NAME`, `FEED_INDEX_NAME` and `INCHES_SHOWN`.
  - **Keys:** reports keep MS1's `pk` and `sk` (`GEO#<cell>`, `SAMPLE` or `EVAL`, then `<time>#<reportId>`). `limits` has one key, `pk`: `{bucket}#{sha256}#{YYYY-MM-DDTHH}`, `{bucket}#ALL#{YYYY-MM-DDTHH}` and `flag#{reportId}#{sha256}`, each item with `expiresAt`.
  - **Read by other milestones** (MS7's Task 0): the output `FeedApiUrl`, the codes above, the template loader `backend/scripts/cfn_template.py`, and the overlay's words in `overlay.py`.
