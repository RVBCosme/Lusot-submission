It gives every fact about the world in the three submission drafts ([README](../drafts/ms8/readme-outline.md), [write-up](../drafts/ms8/write-up-outline.md), [video script](../drafts/ms8/video-script-outline.md)) a public source that a judge can follow. Earlier checks ([Oct 27 plan-update check](2026-10-27-plan-update-check.md), [alert index](2026-10-26-alert-index.md), [verification notes](2026-10-26-verification-notes.md)) were reused as leads, and every source below was opened again on Nov 1.

**How it was checked.**
- "Opened" means the page was fetched on Nov 1, 2026 and the sentence confirmed. Where the fetch tool's summary was unclear (the Aug 9 GMA page, the hackathon rules, four arXiv papers), I read the page's own text or the downloaded PDF.
- "Seen through search" means the site blocked the fetch (HTTP 403) or the page would not load, so I found it with a web search and read the search result's excerpt.
- No Facebook, X, Instagram, TikTok or YouTube page was opened. MMDA's and Quezon City's lists are cited through the GMA News article that carries them.
- No MDPI figure is used (C14).
- Links come from the plan's source list or from a web search. Plan text is not copied here.

## Gaps and corrections

1. **49 flood-prone areas (rows 1 to 3).** The count is from July 2025: ABS-CBN's date is Jul 14, 2025, and I found no newer list. The drafts' present tense ("Metro Manila has 49 ...") should carry the year. Suggested: "In July 2025, MMDA identified 49 flood-prone areas in the National Capital Region." ABS-CBN, the source the drafts name, blocks the fetch, so a judge's browser may open it but I saw it only through search. Philstar (Filipino) and Daily Tribune open and carry the same number; the video's source line could credit one of them as well. **Applied to the drafts, Nov 1.**
2. **More than 500 incidents within 24 hours (rows 4 to 6).** The drafts' wording matches GMA. Make it exact by naming the day and the reporter: "On Jul 22, 2025, MMDA reported more than 500 flood incidents in Metro Manila in the last 24 hours (GMA News)". Three cautions: ABS-CBN's headline has no window; the Tribune says "areas", not "incidents"; and 273 was a later snapshot at 11 a.m. Do not pair 500 with 273 as one statistic. The plan's older phrase "during the July 2025 monsoon" stays out (C14). **Applied to the drafts, Nov 1.**
3. **Aug 9, 2026 lists (rows 7 and 8).**
   - The 4 spots and the 38 entries are correct for the afternoon lists. The morning lists in the alert index come from a different GMA page ("LIVE UPDATES", 8:20 AM and 6:30 AM) and must not be mixed in.
   - The MMDA list is split on the page by a "related stories" block. The three Manila entries follow it and belong to the same 2:02 PM list, but the page does not repeat "according to MMDA" for them.
