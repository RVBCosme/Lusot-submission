# Alert index: public MMDA and city flood lists (search aid for Set A)

These lists were built from a handful of public GMA News articles, read like a person would. Per decision D2, there is no access to social platforms. Use them to find **MMDA or city posts with photos** at these spots and times. Search for those posts by hand (see `docs/data/set-a-collection-guide.md`).

Labels are copied exactly as published. Mapping them to Lusot's levels happens only in `eval/rules.md`.

**Finder aid only (plan §11, as updated Oct 27).** Set A uses only photos that MMDA or a city government posted itself. Photos from these news articles are never used.

## What these lists teach the label mapping (folded into `eval/rules.md` v0.2)

- MMDA sometimes gives **inches** alone ("10"") or with words ("Above gutter deep, 9""), and sometimes a **range** ("13-18 inches").
- Passability phrases go beyond the three codes: "passable to light vehicles", "not passable to all types of vehicles", and Pasig's "not passable to motorcycles and cars (sedan)".
- Field passability can differ from the gauge table. "Waist Deep, 37"" was marked *not passable to light vehicles*, although the gauge says NPATV. This confirms the plan's rule to score level and passability separately.
- "Subsided" is a status, not a depth.
