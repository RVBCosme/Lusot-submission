Its one water record has a 700 × 1600 box and a 448 × 1024 raster, the box scaled by exactly 0.64, so the longer side is 1024. The client requires a raster the size of its box, so it raised `a SAM mask doesn't fill its box inside the image`.

  - **Fix:** C90 (under "Decided"), built test-first by an implementer (Opus 5.5 at `xhigh`) and reviewed before the deploy, so the image carries it. Window 2 heard through the tracker, since MS2's tooling calls the same `segment`.
