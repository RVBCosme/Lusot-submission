## Window 4: the shared day cap on paid analyses (Codex finding 3)

- **What (10:19 AM):** `backend/api/src/limits.ts` charges every upload, sample run and chosen photo against one counter, `analysis#ALL#{UTC date}`, capped at `ANALYSIS_DAY` = 300. It uses the same conditional write as the other counters, and every count taken is given back when a day refuses. An upload refused by a day gets `global_cap`, the only cap the Report screen has a line for; a sample run or chosen photo gets `daily_cap`. API only, so `freeze-1` stays valid. The tests were written first; 7 failed before the code.
- **Review (Opus, one pass):** no correctness bugs in `take()`. It raised one design issue: an upload link costs nothing to ask for, and uploads had no day of their own, while their hour in all (300) equalled the shared day. So one burst of links could shut chosen photos and samples out until UTC midnight. Fixed: uploads now have a day of 100, below the shared 300, as are chosen photos (120) and samples (150). The hour's cap of 300 uploads is now out of reach within a day; it is left as it is. The review's missing test, the sample route's shared-day refusal, was added.
- **The number:** 300 is Window 4's proposal (D7 default). At about $0.03 an analysis, that is about $9 a day at most, under the spend stop.
