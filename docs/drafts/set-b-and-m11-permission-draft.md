## Set B: the car owner's OK

### English

Hi [name]! For my flood-depth app Lusot, built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon, may I photograph your parked car from the side, in daylight, and its tire's sidewall code? I'd use the photos only to test how well Lusot measures depth from wheels.

- I'll keep people out of the frame. Faces are blurred automatically before Meta receives a photo, but automatic blurring can miss some.
- Your plate stays visible. The photos go to Meta's AI service, paid with my hackathon credits, to outline the wheels. Meta may keep them with no fixed period, and may use them to improve its products.
- An AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) helps me check the photos, so Anthropic receives some of them too, under Anthropic's own terms. Anthropic keeps what it receives for 30 days, or up to 2 years if its systems flag it for a usage-policy check.
- I keep the photos privately. They never appear in the app, the demo video or the code repository, and I delete my copies by [date, after the sealed test archive is deleted].

If that's OK, please reply: "Yes, you may use the photos of my car for Lusot's tests."
