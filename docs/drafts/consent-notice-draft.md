## English

**Before your first photo**

Lusot estimates the depth of a flood at one spot from a photo you take.

By continuing, you agree to this:

- **Your photo goes to Meta.** Lusot first blurs faces on its own server, though it can miss one. Then it sends the photo to Meta's AI service to read the water. Licence plates and the rest of the photo are not blurred before this, so Meta receives them. Meta may use what it receives to improve its products. Lusot uses photos only to estimate depth at one spot and show that result.
- [if store: false works] Lusot asks Meta not to store your photo, except when Meta's service is slow. Meta's terms still let it keep what it receives for as long as it needs.
- [if Meta stores photos] Meta keeps copies of the face-blurred photo for as long as it needs them.
- **Lusot blurs faces and plates before it keeps a copy.** Your original waits in a private area until the analysis ends, and is then deleted. Lusot keeps the blurred copy and the report for 7 days.
- **A reading inside Metro Manila becomes a public pin,** on the map and in Lusot's public pin feed, which other flood maps can copy. For up to 3 hours, anyone can see its spot, the time you took the photo, the level, the landmark it was read from, and its passability class. [In the app, the pin also shows the blurred photo with Lusot's outlines (PRIV-Q2).] A photo taken outside Metro Manila never becomes a pin.
- **Questions to Ask Lusot, typed or spoken, also go to Meta,** which may use them to improve its products. [Lusot doesn't keep them (PRIV-Q3).]
- **No accounts, no names.** Lusot uses a random id made on your device, and never writes your location into its logs.

- [ ] I am 18 or older. *(required)*

**[Continue]** · **[Not now]**
