## English

**Before your first photo**

Lusot estimates the depth of a flood at one spot from a photo you take.

By continuing, you agree to this:

- **Your photo goes to Meta.** Lusot first blurs faces automatically on its own server. Automatic blurring can miss some faces: very small, side-on, partly covered or in the dark. Then Lusot sends the photo to Meta's AI service to read the water. Licence plates aren't blurred before Meta receives the photo, only before Lusot stores it, and the rest of the photo isn't blurred either. Meta may keep what it receives with no fixed period, and may use it to improve its products. Lusot uses photos only to estimate depth at one spot and show that result.
- [if store: false works] Lusot asks Meta not to store your photo[, except briefly when Meta's service is slow (if store: false fails in Meta's slow-service mode)]. Meta may still keep what it receives.
- [if Meta stores photos] Meta's outlining service also stores a copy of each request, about 7 per photo.
- **Lusot blurs faces and plates before it keeps a copy,** using Meta's outlines, which can also miss some. Your original waits in a private storage area until the analysis ends, and is then deleted. Lusot keeps the report, and a blurred copy when it reads a level, for 7 days. That covers only Lusot's own copies, not what Meta keeps.
- **A reading inside Metro Manila becomes a public pin,** on the map and in Lusot's public pin feed, which other flood maps can copy. For up to 3 hours, anyone can see its spot, the time you took the photo, the level, the landmark it was read from, and its passability class. [In the app, the pin also shows the blurred photo with Lusot's outlines (PRIV-Q2).] A photo taken outside Metro Manila never becomes a pin.
- **Questions to Ask Lusot also go to Meta:** the typed text, or up to 15 seconds of audio for a spoken question. Meta may keep them with no fixed period, and may use them to improve its products. Lusot doesn't keep the audio [or your typed questions (PRIV-Q3)].
- **No accounts, no names.** Lusot uses a random id made on your device. Its service logs never hold your location or IP address, but a failed upload's queue record keeps your IP address for up to 7 days.

- [ ] I am 18 or older. *(required)*

**[Continue]** · **[Not now]**
