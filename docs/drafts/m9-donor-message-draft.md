## English

Hi [name]! I'm building Lusot, a flood-depth app, for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon. May I use your flood [photo / video] from [place], [date]? Did you take it yourself? If not, who did?

If you agree, I'll use it in these four ways:

1. **Meta's AI service analyses it,** once at first, and again each time the app runs it as a sample.
   - Faces are blurred automatically first, but automatic blurring can miss some faces: very small, side-on, partly covered or in the dark.
   - Plate numbers aren't blurred before Meta receives it.
   - Meta may keep what it receives with no fixed period, and while my hackathon credits pay for the analysis, Meta may use it to improve its products.
   - An AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) may help me check it, so Anthropic may receive it too, under Anthropic's own terms. Anthropic keeps what it receives for 30 days, or up to 2 years if its systems flag it for a usage-policy check.
2. **The Lusot app shows it,** as a sample and as a labelled demo pin, for as long as the app runs, with faces and plate numbers blurred.
3. **My demo video and a post on X** use it, blurred the same way.
4. **I submit it to the hackathon.** The organizers, their subsidiaries, agents and partner companies get a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to use what's submitted for any purpose, including marketing.

Faces and plate numbers are blurred before anything is shown, though automatic blurring can miss some. I check each one by eye before it's shown.

Who appears in it, and did they agree too? Where exactly, and at what time, was it taken?

If you agree, please reply: "Yes, you may use it for all four." You can say no, or withdraw any time before Nov 3. After Nov 3 I can still take down the sample and the demo pin, but not the video, the post or the submitted copy. Withdrawing can't recall copies Meta already holds.
